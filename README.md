# Understanding

An all-in-one Roblox menu that works in every game. One window, a search box that finds any
feature, and it updates itself.

**Discord:** https://discord.gg/h4R9W7gT2H (updates, help and bug reports)

## Run it

Paste this into your executor:

```lua
loadstring(game:HttpGet("https://raw.githubusercontent.com/davvood/Understanding-release/refs/heads/main/loader.lua"))()
```

Press **C** to open and close the menu. You can change the key in Settings.

## What it can do

**Visuals**
- Player ESP: boxes, names, distance, health, skeleton, tracers, chams, off-screen arrows
- A live preview on your own avatar where you drag the labels into place
- Object ESP, radar, crosshair, watermark, keybind list, list of active features

**Movement**
- Fly, Air Walk, Noclip, Infinite Jump, walk speed and jump power
- Vehicle tab for any car you drive: speed boost, nitro, drift, grip, turn assist, jump, gravity, speedometer
- Follow Player: walks after a friend and finds its way around walls
- Teleports tab: your saved places, NPCs and items in one searchable list; teleport there or walk there

**Emotes and animations**
- About 60,000 emotes with search, folders and keybinds
- Animation packs, emote speed, keep emoting while you move

**Camera and world**
- Freecam, camera through walls, field of view, X-Ray
- Fullbright, sky presets, time of day, personal light
- Click to delete parts, Performance Mode, Loud Sound Limiter

**Players and servers**
- Spectate, teleport to players, chat log, join and leave alerts
- Server browser, server hop, rejoin where you stood

**Automations**
- Your own rules: "when my health drops below 30%, teleport to Base", "when a Golden Chest appears, tell me"
- Triggers for health, items and NPCs, players joining, respawning, keys, timers and vehicles

**Comfort**
- A keybind for everything (right-click a toggle), plus a panic key
- Configs you can save, load and share
- Dark and light theme, accent colour, Streamer Mode

## Good to know

- **Executors:** built and tested on Saiko. It should run on other executors too; the menu tells
  you on the first start which features yours can't run.
- **Your settings** are saved in your executor's workspace folder (files starting with
  `Understanding_`).
- **Updates** arrive by themselves: the loader always gets the newest version, and the menu offers
  a reload when a new one is published while you play.
- **Use it at your own risk.** Scripts are against Roblox's rules and can get an account banned.
